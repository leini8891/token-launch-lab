# TGE Spec: Sample Launch For Red-Team Demo

## Project

Name: HarborUSD

Category: stablecoin settlement and treasury workflow for APAC exporters

## Launch Goal

HarborUSD wants to launch a governance token, HARBOR, to bootstrap liquidity, reward early users, and coordinate future protocol upgrades.

## Token Supply

Total supply: 1,000,000,000 HARBOR

Initial circulating supply at TGE: 12%

## Allocation

- Team: 20%, 12-month cliff, 36-month linear vesting
- Investors: 18%, 6-month cliff, 24-month linear vesting
- Community incentives: 30%, released through campaigns over 48 months
- Treasury: 22%, controlled by a 3-of-5 multisig
- Liquidity and market making: 10%, available at TGE

## Contract Plan

- ERC20 governance token
- Vesting contracts for team and investors
- Treasury multisig controls incentive emissions
- Emergency pause policy is not finalized
- External audit is planned after the prototype

## Distribution

- Private round investors include funds in Singapore, Korea, and the US
- Community campaign may include airdrops, referrals, and trading rewards
- Public sale is being considered but not yet designed

## Narrative

HarborUSD is "the Stripe Atlas for stablecoin settlement."

The team claims the token gives users governance, ecosystem rewards, and alignment with protocol growth.

## Known Concerns From Founder

- Wants to avoid looking like another points-to-token farm
- Wants CEX listing conversations within six months
- Wants to avoid investor unlock drama
- Wants launch messaging that can survive crypto Twitter scrutiny

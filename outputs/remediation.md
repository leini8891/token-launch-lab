# Remediation

## Recovery / Revision Loop

- No report revisions required.

## P0: Fix Before Mainnet, Listing, Or Public Campaigns

- REG-001: Get qualified legal review, define jurisdiction gating, investor eligibility, transfer restrictions, and communications policy before any public sale decision.
- DUMP-001: Model month-by-month unlock pressure, publish a transparent unlock calendar, and consider longer lockups or staggered unlock tranches before listing conversations.
- PROTO-001: Define pause authority, multisig policy, event disclosure process, and post-incident restart criteria. Keep this as defensive readiness planning only.
- CT-002: Publish user workflows, non-speculative utility, and retention metrics before token reward campaigns.

## P1: Fix Before Public Launch Materials

- PROTO-002: Separate prototype status from production readiness, publish audit status honestly, and require all high-severity audit items to close before TGE.
- CT-001: Clarify why the token is necessary, what users can do without speculation, and what measurable network behavior the token coordinates.
- DUMP-002: Document market-maker mandate, inventory limits, reporting cadence, and conflict controls before public launch materials.
- REG-002: Review campaign mechanics with qualified counsel and compliance reviewers; avoid messaging that implies returns or encourages manipulative trading behavior.
